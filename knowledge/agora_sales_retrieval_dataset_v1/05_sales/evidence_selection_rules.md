# Evidence selection rules

## Internal evidence score
- Prospect relevance: 0–5
- Korea locality: 0–3
- Recency: 0–2
- Evidence strength: 0–3
- Quantified outcome: 0–2

## Email budget
Quality:
- evidence max 2
- numeric metrics max 2

Normal:
- evidence max 1
- numeric metric max 1

## Korea-first examples
Education: Hanyang → EnglishCentral
AIoT: XOsoft → Miko
Luxury retail: prospect Korea research → Salesroom / relevant product evidence → Q2 live-shopping signal

## Attribution
Wrong: “Agora를 쓰면 verification이 58% 빨라집니다.”
Right: “Agora 공개 Kriyam 사례에서는 평균 call duration이 19분에서 8분으로 감소했습니다.”

## MOU
MOU = market direction/partnership evidence.
MOU ≠ ROI/production success evidence.
