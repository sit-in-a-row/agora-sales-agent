# Sales Strategist / Writer context contract

## Prospect block
- company
- Korea business context
- department
- title/seniority
- visit group
- attendee-stated interests

## Account Research
- recent verified signals max 3
- source URL
- confidence

## Agora retrieval
- products max 3
- cases max 2
- claims max 3
- claims_and_guardrails.md mandatory

## Strategist output
- customer hypothesis
- evidence-backed fit
- product angle
- why now
- proof
- CTA
- forbidden claims

## Writer may
- shorten/reorder
- adapt Korean tone
- connect verified prospect facts with approved Agora evidence

## Writer may not
- research new facts
- invent metrics
- turn hypothesis into fact
- turn MOU/pilot/roadmap into commercial success
- imply another customer's KPI will repeat

## Email logic
1. event/visit context
2. attendee-stated interest
3. prospect recent signal
4. relevant Agora capability
5. nearest proof point
6. low-friction CTA
